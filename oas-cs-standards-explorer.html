Notes about these standards
  • Transcribed from the Oklahoma Academic Standards for Computer Science (February 2023). Check the official document from the Oklahoma State Department of Education for the authoritative text.
  • Standard IDs read as grade, concept, subconcept, number. For example, 3.AP.PD.02 is Grade 3, Algorithms & Programming, Program Development, standard 02. High school IDs start with L1 (Grades 9–10) or L2 (Grades 11–12).
  • The order of standards within a grade is not a teaching sequence.
  • Grades 6–8 Variables, High School Level 2 Devices, and High School Level 2 Social Interactions have no new standards. Students continue to apply the standards and practices from earlier grade levels.
  • Additional Modularity and Culture standards begin in Grade 3. Additional Social Interactions standards begin in Grade 3. Additional Algorithms standards begin in High School Level 2.
  • The practices listed on each standard are the ones the document connects to that standard. Where the document shows one practice line under a group of grades, it is applied to each grade in the group.
  • The Progression view lists standards that share the same concept, subconcept, and number across grades. Where the document adds standards at a later grade (such as Modularity .02 in Grade 3), the progression starts there.
